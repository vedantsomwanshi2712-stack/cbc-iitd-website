## About
## A student club for building with Claude.
Claude Builder Clubs are Anthropic's official student-led campus communities. This is the one at IIT Delhi.

### Open to every branch and year
First year or final year, any department. You don't need to be from CS.

### For beginners and experienced coders
Never written code? We start from the first step. Already shipping? Bring your project.

### Build what you care about
A tool for your hostel, a helper for a course, a script for your research. You choose.

### Meet people who build
Meetups, workshops and talks on campus, with time to build next to each other.

## What we do
## Meetups, workshops and a hackathon.

### Monthly meetups
Every month. Learn one thing, build for a while, then show what you made.

### Hands-on workshops
Two a term. Claude Code and Skills first, then Building Agents, Building with MCP and an AI Research Salon.

### Campus-wide hackathon
In spring. Build something with Claude and demo it to the campus.

### Campus sessions
A 10-minute demo for your club, class or hostel. Invite us

## FAQ
## Questions before you join.

Do I need to be from CS?
No. The club is open to every branch and every year.

Does it cost anything?
No, it's free.

How do I get the API credits?
Make an account on platform.claude.com with your IIT Delhi email. Then, at a meetup, fill in the check-in form with your Org ID (Settings → Organization).

What happens at a meetup?
A short talk or workshop, time to build, and members showing what they made.

Is this official?
It's a student-led club, part of Anthropic's Claude Builder Club program.

How do I join?
Fill in the join form. Join the club
