A student club for building with Claude.

Claude is an AI model made by Anthropic. Claude Builder Clubs are Anthropic's official student-led campus communities, and this is the one at IIT Delhi.

  • Open to students of every branch and year

    First year or final year, any department. You don't need to be from CS.

  • A club for beginners and experienced coders

    Never written code? We start from the first step. Already shipping? Bring your project.

  • Projects you care about

    A tool for your hostel, a helper for a course, a script for your research. You choose what to build.

  • Meetups, workshops and talks

    Meet other students who are building things, and work on your projects together.
